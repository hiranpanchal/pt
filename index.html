How It Works

The Lee Hayward
Approach

Three things that make the difference between people who see results and people who don't.

01

Structure & Consistency

Results come from showing up and doing the work, week after week. I build programmes that fit your life so you can actually stick to them — not just for a month, but long term.

02

Proper Training

Compound lifts, progressive overload, and training that's right for your body and your goals. Whether you want to lose fat, build muscle, or both — I write the plan and coach you through it.

03

Session Data

Every session comes with detailed tracking so you can see exactly how you're progressing. Lee records key measurements including weight, BMI, and body fat percentage — giving you clear, objective data to show what's working and keep you motivated as your body changes.
